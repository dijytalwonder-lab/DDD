Diwali Delivery Dash — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026

Developer: DijytalWonder

Contact: dijytalwonder@gmail.com

Diwali Delivery Dash does not collect any personal information. The only information the app keeps is stored locally on your own device and includes your progression (levels and stars), coins and hearts, your best Endless-mode score, and your sound settings.

Data Practices

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any user information. It contains no advertising networks, no analytics systems, and no tracking mechanisms. It does not ask for the camera, microphone, contacts, photos, location, or any other sensitive permission.

Optional Google Sign-In

The app may show an optional “Sign in with Google” button, intended to let players save progress across devices in a future update. This feature is not active in the current version and does not collect any Google account information. You can always continue as a guest and play without signing in.

Storage Details

Game progress remains exclusively on your device within the app’s private storage area. This data can be removed at any time by uninstalling the application or by clearing its data through your device’s settings.

Children’s Privacy

Diwali Delivery Dash is family-friendly and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. Because no personal information is collected, no dedicated child-specific data protections are necessary.

User Control

You retain full control of your saved data and can clear it through your device’s settings at any time. Android’s own backup system may include your game progress if you have that feature enabled; this is stored in your personal Google Drive and the developer has no access to it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at dijytalwonder@gmail.com.
