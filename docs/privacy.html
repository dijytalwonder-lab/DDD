Privacy Policy

Diwali Delivery Dash · DijytalWonder · Last updated: 29 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Diwali Delivery Dash (“the App”, package com.dijytal.diwalideliverydash), published by DijytalWonder (“we”, “us”), handles information. We built the App to respect your privacy: it is designed to work fully offline and does not collect personal information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal or sensitive user data. The App has no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Data stored on your device

The App saves your game progress locally on your device only — for example your level stars, coins, hearts, best Endless-mode score, and sound settings. This information stays on your device, is never uploaded to us or any third party, and is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Optional Google Sign-In

The App may present an optional “Sign in with Google” button intended to let players sync progress across devices in the future. This feature is not active in the current version and does not collect any Google account information. If we enable it in a future update, we will update this policy and use your Google account identifier solely to save your own game progress; you can always continue as a guest instead.

Permissions

The App requests the INTERNET permission, which is a standard requirement of the app framework used to load the game’s own content. The App does not use it to send your personal data anywhere.

Children’s privacy

The App is family-friendly and suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else, since the App does not collect personal information at all.

Data deletion

Because all data is stored locally, you can delete it at any time by clearing the App’s storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at dijytalwonder@gmail.com.
